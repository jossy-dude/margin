## 3. `p` — what it means now, and the truth table

`p` is **percent remaining of the active class period**, from real clock time, in
`src/ui/instruments/time.ts`. `p = 1` means the class has not started. `p = 0` means it is over.

| phase | shows | `p` | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `before` | the first class | **1** | you have all of it |
| `running` | that class, counting | `(end − now) / (end − start)` | the only honest reading |
| `gap` | the class that **just ended** | **0** | not the next one at 1 |
| `over` | the last class | **0** | |

The `gap` row is the decision that matters. It is tempting to show the *upcoming* class at 1 during a
free period. That is the exact lie this rewrite exists to remove: a bar that snaps back to full the
instant a class ends is a bar that lies at the most legible moment of the day. The app's own `ticker`
kind answers "how long until the next one", and it is kept, so the question is not lost — it is just
not answered by the countdown.

**The count: 22 new instruments, in 6 groups. Plus the app's own 4, kept. Settings will show 26.**
Not 20 — `sightglass` and `ticker` exist in the reference *and* in the app, so I named mine after the
machine instead (`graduated` / "Gauge glass", `scope` / "Oscilloscope", `tape` / "Market tape") and
kept both. Renaming an existing kind is forbidden and I did not do it; a picker showing two things
called "Ticker" would be a bug, so the collision is resolved on my side of the line. A test asserts
no id and no name collides across all 26.

## 4. Two inks, and where the accent is allowed

The reference paints every instrument in a bay's grey and a bay's white and spends **no accent at
all** in the art — the accent there is on the scrub and the transport key, outside the drawing. I kept
that rule and enforced it: a shape declares `rest`, `live`, or `signal`, and nothing else is
reachable. 22 × 5 widths × 6 p × 2 palettes, every `fill=`/`stroke=` in the output is one of three
values. A test asserts it.

`signal` — the accent — is spent on **the mover and nothing else**: the needle, the hand, the sweep
caret, the playhead, the pulse, the promotion pointer, the drum's current digits. A test asserts no
signal-filled area exceeds 22 px, so nobody later spends the accent on a whole block.

The gate: measured on the command-deck slab, the app's accent is **2.14:1 (refined light), 2.92:1
(original light), 1.86:1 (original dark)** against a 3:1 floor for an informative graphic. Only
refined dark is legible (7.96:1). So `twoInks()` spends the accent only where it clears 3:1 and falls
back to full ink otherwise. The visible consequence: **in three of the four palettes the instruments
are strictly two inks, which is what the reference intends**, and in refined dark the moving part is
accent-coded. If someone later fixes the palettes, the instruments pick the accent up with no code
change. That is `qa/HANDOFF-A2.md` §6.1.

I also removed one of my own violations during the pass: the accent was on the entire lit *run* of
spiral / scope / surplus / compound / tape / sieve / ladder / lattice — eight instruments that were
nearly orange. Colour belongs to the edge that is moving, not to the length behind it.

## 5. Default recommendation: `gauge` (Dial)

**Change `DEFAULT_SETTINGS.bar` from `'spines'` to `'gauge'`.** One character, `qa/HANDOFF-A2.md` §1.
Arguing it from the design, not from taste:

1. **It is the only instrument where the whole period and the part of it left are in the same frame.**
   The scale is engraved once and never moves; the needle and the swept arc move together. That is
   the literal requirement in the reference's own prose ("the whole period is always legible, which is
   what the old clipped texture could not do") and it is the app's actual job in that strip.
2. **It is correct at both ends without a legend.** Needle hard right at 09:00, hard left at the bell.
   `spiral` at p=0 is an empty groove, which is fine but needs a beat of "what am I looking at".
3. **It reads at 351 × 44.** The Spiral is the prettiest thing here and it is the least legible: eight
   turns over 32 px is a line, not a groove. The Dial is a 32-segment arc, 11 ticks, a needle and a
   pivot, and every one of them is ≥ 2 px.
4. **It is the cheapest of the 22** — 46 shapes against `spectrum`'s ~95 and `sieve`'s 2 × n — and it is
   the one bar that is on screen on the Today page. A default has to be cheap.
5. **It cannot be mistaken for a bar chart**, which is the complaint against the current default.
6. **It gives the accent a home.** The needle is the natural mover, so in refined dark — the app's
   own dark default — the default bar is the one instrument where the accent is actually doing work.

Rejected: **`spiral`** — beautiful, unreadable at this size, and its reading depends on path length
rather than time. **`odometer`** — the most literal reading, but the app already prints "until 15:40"
in the line above; a second clock in the strip is redundant, and it is the only one of the 22 that can
be *numerically* wrong. **`graduated`** — the quietest and genuinely lovely, but a horizontal fill in
a rounded rect is the same silhouette as the current default. It fixes the number, not the shape.

## 6. Per-subject assignment: **not in v1**, and here is why

**Decided: user-picked, one global kind, as today.** The reference maps five named demo subjects to
five machines. This app's subjects are user data — arbitrary names, arbitrary counts, courses the
owner typed in. A hardcoded five-subject map would be wrong for essentially every real user, and
auto-assigning by subject name would need a name-matching heuristic that silently picks the wrong
machine.

The six groups are still there, and that is what Part 2 asks for: they are **how the 26 kinds are
organised in the picker**, not a binding to a course. "Works in any subject" is the group that
answers the actual question; the five named groups are three curated takes each.

The v2 slice is written out exactly in `qa/HANDOFF-A2.md` §5 — `barsByCourse?: Record<string,
BarKindId>` on `Settings`, absent-means-global so the migration is a no-op, plus a resolver and a
picker row. It touches **four shared files, two of them owned by agents who are not finished**. That
is why it is v2 and not a one-liner dressed up as a feature.

Flagging it as an owner decision, per the brief: if you want per-subject assignment, say so and it is
a small follow-up. I would not do it before the 22 are selectable at all.

## 7. What each instrument looked like WRONG before I fixed it

Every one of these was found by rendering and looking, not by reading code.

| instrument | what it looked like | why | fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| **`sets`** | three plain circles, **no lens at all** | I built the lens from two arc commands and guessed the sweep flags. Both arcs bulged the same way, so the "intersection" rendered as a crescent | `lensPts()` — the overlap is now sampled from both circles' own arcs |
| **`surplus`** | **two crossing straight lines and a 5 px sliver.** Did not read as economics at all | the reference's exponents (0.9 / 1.15) are nearly linear at this height, and its hardcoded equilibrium of 0.58 put the shaded triangle out of the useful range | exponent 0.55 / 1.70 so both curves are visibly curved, and the equilibrium is now **found by bisection** instead of assumed, so the shaded region is the real triangle |
| **`compound`** | the caption `1.00 to 10.40` sat **on top of** the highest step | caption at `top − 1`, step at `top` | `top` moved 9 → 15, caption to `top − 9` |
| **`lattice`** | **at p = 0, column 0 was fully lit** — a lit column means "some of the period is left" | `i <= f + 1e-6` is true at `f = 0` | guarded on `f > 1e-6`. Caught by the "no lit area at p = 0" test, not by eye — at that size one lit column out of eleven is invisible |
| **`stack`** | **at p = 0, frame 0 was still lit** with the caret on it — the stack never emptied | `Math.max(1, …)` floored the lit count at 1 | no floor; caret clamped at frame 0 |
| **`cassette`** | at p = 1 the **supply reel was nearly empty and the take-up was full** — a transport running backwards | `rs = 3 + 7(1 − p)`, `rt = 3 + 7p`, inverted | `rs = 3 + 7p`, `rt = 3 + 7(1 − p)`. Also removed a `max(39, …)` that made the take-up leg permanently zero-length, so both tangent runs are real and sum to a constant (tested) |
| **`odometer`** | the `:` separator dots landed **inside drum two**, on top of the digit | reference computes `x0 + cw·1.5 + 12`; at any width above ~300 px that is inside the drum | `x0 + cw·2 + 12` — in the 8 px gap. Visible immediately at 351 px |
| **`spectrum`** | a dense comb that **only got shorter at the ends**; the ceiling at p = 1 was a line **no channel ever touched**, so it was reading nothing | the tallest channel happened to be 22 px, not 27 | **peak-normalised** — gain is computed so the tallest channel reaches the ceiling exactly, which is what peak-hold means. `maxh` 27 → 29 |
| **`spectrum`** | the peak-hold flag hung **2.6 px below the axis** once the ceiling fell | a right-pointing triangle centred on the ceiling | points up, from the ceiling line |
| **`sieve`** | one faint diagonal per composite — at 200 px it read as **nothing** | a single 1.2 px stroke does not read as "struck out" | an **X**, 1.3 px, in live ink at 0.72 — the mark now reads at 200 px |
| **`surplus`** | a `SURPLUS` caption sitting on its own dashed price line | | removed. The shape says it; the picker description says it |
| **all `run` shapes** | at p = 0 a **stray dot at the start of the groove** | `dasharray="0 0"` means **solid**, not nothing | zero-length runs emit the resting draw only |
| **8 instruments** | nearly **orange** | I put the accent on the whole lit run | accent moved to the moving edge |

And two structural findings, both from looking rather than from a failing test:

- **All curve commands are gone.** Every path in all 22 instruments is `M` / `L` / `Z` over sampled
  points — zero `A`, `Q`, `T`, `C`. Arc flags, quadratics and cubics all have uneven support in
  `react-native-svg`'s Android path parser and I have no device to verify them against. A sampled
  polyline is exact to a tenth of a pixel at these radii and is the one path form the renderer is known
  to handle. A test asserts the absence of every other command letter.
- **`dominantBaseline` is not used.** `y` is the text's **centre** and the baseline offset is applied
  by hand — the same arithmetic in the React renderer and in the offline one, which is the only reason
  the PNGs are a valid preview of the phone at all.

## 8. What I could NOT verify, and why

**No device. `qa/STATE.md` — pattern lock. This is the single biggest caveat in this report.**

1. **That `react-native-svg` renders these primitives as Chrome does.** I have verified the geometry;
   I have not verified the rasteriser. Specifically unverified on the SM-A516N: `strokeDashoffset` on
   a `Path` driven by `useAnimatedProps` (the UI-thread reveal), `opacity` on a nested `<G>` inside a
   path `<d>`, `textAnchor` on `<SvgText>`, `rx` on a `<Rect>` at 5 px, and `transform` on `<G>` with
   three chained operations (the Venn lens, the chain glyphs).
2. **The typeface.** My offline render substitutes `"Courier New"` for `SpecialElite_400Regular`. The
   digit *widths* in the offline sheets are therefore approximate. Anything sized to fit text — the
   odometer's drum pitch, the tape's price columns, the sieve's numerals — could be a few pixels off
   on the phone. The odometer's drums are the risk: `cw` is derived from `w`, not from the glyph
   advance, and `Special Elite` may be wider than the fallback.
3. **That the 1 Hz tick feels right.** Mathematically a 50-minute period moves a 30 px needle by
   **0.063 px per second**. Linear interpolation between ticks would differ from the truth by at most
   0.00017 of the bar at the midpoint — sub-pixel everywhere. So there is nothing to interpolate and I
   did not build a per-frame loop. That is a calculation, not a feel, and it is the reasoning behind
   the one animation I *did* add (below).
4. **The one animation is untested on device.** The 240 ms ease-out period-change wipe
   (`ENTER_MS` in `InstrumentBar.tsx`) and its `run` dash reveal. Duration and curve are argued in the
   file's docstring; only the code path is verified.
5. **Whether 26 previews in the Settings sheet is fast enough.** Mitigated in the handoff patch by
   making 25 of them static `SvgXml` strings, but a sheet with 26 SVG trees in it has not been timed.
   A1's `qa/RESEARCH-PERF.md` is the right reference for this.
6. **Screen-reader output.** The `accessibilityLabel` is written and asserted in code; that it reads
   sensibly through TalkBack is untested.
7. **The `live={false}` off-screen gate** is implemented and defaulted to `true`; applying it is a
   handoff to A5, so today every call site behaves exactly as before.

**What I am confident about:** the geometry (rendered and looked at at 5 widths × 6 values of `p` ×
2 themes), the `p` semantics (a truth table, not a rendering), the id story (provably zero ids), the
no-stretch story (provably no transform on the root, `width`/`height` *are* the viewBox), the two-ink
rule (provably three colours in the output), and that nothing is on screen that reads wrong at p = 1
or p = 0.
